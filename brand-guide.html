How We Purge the "Claude-Code" Signature

[ 01 // TYPOGRAPHY ] OPTICAL SCALE
Human Editorial vs Standard SaaS Sans
The AI Cliché Defaulting to Inter, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans at standard weights without expressiveness or contrast.
The JV Standard Fraunces optical serif (warm ink traps & fluid italic accents) paired with tightly-tracked geometric grotesque and clean monospaced indexers.
[ 02 // GEOMETRY ] CORNER ARCHITECTURE
Architectural Flush Cards & Soft Control Pills
The AI Cliché Universal 14px–18px rounded corners on every container, softening the interface into an amorphous bubble.
The JV Standard Structural containers feature crisp 4px–6px architectural corners with fine hairline borders. Only floating interactive controls use pill radii.
[ 03 // GLYPHS & ICONOGRAPHY ] INDEXING SYSTEM
Instrument Notations vs Emojis in Boxes
The AI Cliché Placing emojis (🦅, 🏆, 📊, 🤖, ✨) inside rounded square boxes as pseudo-icons.
The JV Standard Bracketed coordinate markers ([ 01 ], [ REC ], § 4.2), status signal dots, and bespoke hairline SVG meters.
